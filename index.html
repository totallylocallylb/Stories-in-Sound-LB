About the Project & Why Now

Every Street Has a Story Worth Keeping

Leighton Buzzard has grown and changed remarkably over the last few decades. While historic buildings like our Market Cross and the Grand Union Canal remain at the heart of town, the personal memories of working life, childhood games, bustling Tuesday markets, and local character can easily fade if nobody sits down to press record.

100% Independent & Local

Started independently by a fellow resident with a passion for local heritage—not a corporate or council top-down initiative. Built by neighbours, for neighbours.

Everyday Voices Matter

You don’t need to be famous to have an important story. From sand quarry workers and canal boaters to shopkeepers, teachers, and families who moved here recently—all voices belong.

Captured Before It’s Lost

Written facts tell us dates, but recorded voices carry laughter, regional accents, and genuine emotion—creating a living sound archive for future generations in LU7.

Two Ways to Get Involved

Whether you have decades of Leighton Buzzard memories to share or want to help gather stories across town, we’d love to welcome you.

For Residents of All Ages

1. Share Your Story

Have you lived or worked in Leighton Buzzard, Linslade, or the surrounding villages? Sit down for a relaxed, friendly chat over a cup of tea and tell us about the town as you know it.

  • ✓ No preparation needed: It’s a natural conversation, not an exam.
  • ✓ Your comfort first: Recorded in your own home or a quiet local spot like the library or a community café.
  • ✓ Full control: You approve what gets shared publicly and receive a personal copy of your recording.

2. Become a Volunteer Interviewer

I’m looking for curious, good-humoured locals to join me in conducting, recording, and transcribing interviews across the town so we can reach every neighbourhood.

  • ✓ Great for students, retirees & history fans: Meet fascinating neighbours and build real oral history skills.
